# Audit Log

Aggregated record of every audit flag and every ambiguity raised against a supplied
specification. Entries here are annotations only. No supplied rule is ever altered,
corrected, or removed on the basis of an entry in this file.

- **Audit flag** — a supplied rule that appears technically incorrect, internally
  inconsistent, or in conflict with another supplied rule. The rule stands as supplied.
- **Ambiguity** — a supplied rule that is under-determined, so more than one reading is
  possible. Recorded as an open question, never resolved by inference.
